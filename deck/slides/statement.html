THE HEADLINE

Our model says 81%.
With IPCC-sized uncertainty it says 67%.
Kalshi says 70%.

The gap isn’t about where warming is heading. It’s about how sure we can be.

This is the one slide to remember. Our ensemble of the class model says about 81% chance of a 2°C year before 2050. But when we widen the model’s uncertainty to match the IPCC’s ranges, the same model says 67%, which is almost exactly what the market says, 70%. So there are two ways to read it. If you trust the class model’s narrow view, the market is too low and Yes is cheap. If you think the IPCC’s wider uncertainty is right, which most climate scientists would, the market is fairly priced. Every version of our model agrees on the middle, about 2°C around 2045 to 2050. They differ only on how confident to be.
