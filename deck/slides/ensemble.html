This is how we get our headline number. We built it the way a modeller would: run the class model many times while changing its most important settings, and average. We changed four things: climate sensitivity (Parameter 2), how fast the atmosphere adjusts (Parameter 1), the emissions path (the HW5 economy, falling, flat or rising), and whether the land-use emissions the Kaya block leaves out are included. That makes 72 combinations, 4,000 futures each. The table shows how much each setting moves the answer. Climate sensitivity matters most, from 58% to 98%. Emissions matter less than you might expect, because most warming before 2050 is already locked in. Mixing all 72 together gives the curve on the right. Its mean says 80.8% chance of a 2°C year before 2050, about 11 points above Kalshi.
