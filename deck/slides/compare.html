Every version of the model next to Kalshi

Yellow band: the range Kalshi’s price allows once its fee and the bid–ask spread are counted (66–74%).

Here are all the headline numbers side by side. Kalshi’s price is 70%, and once you count Kalshi’s fee and the gap between the buy and sell prices, any belief between 66% and 74% is consistent with it. That’s the yellow band. Our IPCC-calibrated model, 67%, sits inside the band: it agrees with the market. Our 72-version ensemble, 81%, sits above it. Fitted to history, 93%, and the class model exactly as taught, 99%, are well above. The more confident the version, the higher the probability. That’s because the middle of every version is already a bit above 2°C, so a narrower distribution puts more of its weight above the line.
