STRATEGY A · HYPOTHETICAL

Value bet: buy Yes if we believe 81%

Cost incl. fee

73.4¢

Edge

+7.4¢

  • $1,000 buys 1,362 contracts
  • 2°C reached: +$362. Not reached: −$1,000
  • Expected: +$100 over ~17 years, plus Kalshi’s 3.5% interest
  • With the IPCC’s 67%, the same bet expects −6.3¢ a contract
The simplest strategy: if you believe our 81% and the market charges 73.4 cents including the fee, buying Yes has an expected profit of 7.4 cents per contract. With a hypothetical $1,000 you’d buy 1,362 contracts. If any year hits 2°C before 2050 you collect $1,362, a profit of $362. If not, you lose the $1,000. The expected profit is about $100. But it takes on average 17 years to pay, so per year it’s small, plus Kalshi pays 3.5% interest on the position in the meantime. The Kelly formula would put 28% of a bankroll on it; half of that is the usual cautious version. The chart shows the catch. The bet is only profitable if your probability is above the 73.4% cost. Our ensemble says 81%, so yes. Our own IPCC-calibrated estimate says 67%, and on that view the bet loses about 6 cents a contract. So the trade depends entirely on which version of our model you trust.
