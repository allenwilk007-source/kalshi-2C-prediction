LIMITATIONS & NEXT STEPS

What the model can’t do (yet)

Limitations

  • CO₂ only: aerosols and methane are folded into Parameter 2
  • No El Niño forecasts, which the market seems to use
  • Damage estimates vary hugely across studies
  • Back-tests assume orders fill; real fills are harder

Next steps

  • Add non-CO₂ forcing and ENSO forecasts
  • Keep tracking model vs market as data arrives
  • Extend the scanner to new climate contracts

To be honest about the limits. The class model only tracks CO₂, so the effects of aerosols and methane are hidden inside Parameter 2. That’s exactly why we needed the IPCC-calibrated version. The model knows nothing about El Niño forecasts, which the market clearly uses. Damage estimates in the literature range from small benefits to losses of more than 20% of GDP, so any money figure for climate damage is very uncertain. And our trading back-tests assume orders get filled at the prices we chose, which is optimistic in a thin market. Next steps would be to add non-CO₂ forcing and seasonal El Niño forecasts, keep tracking how our model and the market move as new data arrives (the dashboard already records every run), and extend the scanner as Kalshi lists new climate contracts. Everything here is a hypothetical class analysis. We didn’t place any trades, and none of it is investment advice. Everything is public on GitHub at github.com/allenwilk007-source/kalshi-2C-prediction, so you can check any number yourself. Thank you.
