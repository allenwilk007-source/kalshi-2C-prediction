MODEL VS MARKET

Where the model’s futures land

Each curve is 100,000 simulated futures. The area to the right of the 2.00°C line is the chance the contract pays Yes.

This is the heart of the project. Each curve is a probability density: where one version of the model’s 100,000 futures land. On the x-axis is the highest reading the contract will see between 2027 and 2049. The contract pays Yes exactly when that peak is at or above 2.00°C, so the area to the right of the yellow line is the probability of Yes. Look at the shapes. The class model as taught (purple) is tall and narrow and sits almost entirely right of 2°C: 99%. Fitted to history (blue) is a bit wider: 93%. The IPCC-calibrated version (red) has about the same middle, around 2.1°C, but a much wider spread, so a third of it falls below 2°C: 67%. So the versions agree on where warming is headed. They disagree on how sure we can be.
