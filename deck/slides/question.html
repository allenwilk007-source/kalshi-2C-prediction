THE BET

Kalshi’s contract KXWARMING-50

  • Pays $1 if any single calendar year before 2050 is at least 2.00°C above the 1850–1900 average
  • At least 2 of 4 official datasets must show it: NASA GISS, NOAA, Berkeley Earth, HadCRUT
  • 2024, the warmest year so far, was about 1.4–1.6°C depending on the dataset
  • A small market: about 4,100 contracts open, a few trades a day

Kalshi is a prediction market regulated by the CFTC. Each contract pays one dollar if the event happens and nothing if it doesn’t, so the price in cents reads as the market’s probability. This contract is unusual in three ways that matter for modelling. First, it is about a single year, not a 20-year average the way the IPCC defines crossing 2°C, so a hot El Niño year could trigger it early. Second, two of the four datasets have to agree, and the datasets differ by about a tenth of a degree, so in practice the second-highest dataset decides it. Third, it uses the 1850–1900 baseline, so we had to put every dataset and our model on that baseline. On 18 November 2026 you could buy Yes for 72 cents and sell for 68, so the midpoint is 70%. The market is thin: about 4,100 contracts open and only a handful of trades a day.
