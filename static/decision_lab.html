Would you bet on a 2°C year before 2050?

On Kalshi, a regulated prediction market, you can bet on whether any single year before 2050 will be at least 2°C warmer than pre-industrial times. The price is the crowd’s probability. This tool gives you a second opinion: a climate model (emissions → carbon cycle → temperature) connected to real temperature data and run hundreds of thousands of times. Take the model’s recommendation, or build your own forecast and see how your odds compare with the market’s.
