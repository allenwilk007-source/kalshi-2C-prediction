Would you bet on a 2°C year before 2050?

Kalshi, a regulated prediction market, lets people bet on whether any single year before 2050 will be at least 2°C warmer than pre-industrial times. Its price is the crowd’s probability. We rebuilt the climate model from our class (Kaya → carbon cycle → temperature), connected it to real data and ran it hundreds of thousands of times to check that price. Now it’s your turn: take our call, or build your own forecast.
