Will any year reach 2°C above pre-industrial before 2050?

Kalshi contract KXWARMING-50 pays $1 if, in any calendar year up to 2049, at least two of NASA, NOAA, Berkeley Earth and HadCRUT report ≥ 2.00°C above 1850–1900. This page runs the Carbon Trading class model (HW2–HW5) forward with uncertainty and compares its probability with the market's.
