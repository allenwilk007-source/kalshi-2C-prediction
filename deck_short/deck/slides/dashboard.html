THE DASHBOARD

How the dashboard works

1 · Live dashboard. Pulls Kalshi’s price, reruns the model, and shows the two numbers side by side

2 · Decision Lab. Take our recommendation (Path A) or build your own forecast (Path B)

3 · Your result. Your chance vs Kalshi’s, the PDF of your futures, and a Yes, No or Pass call

We built a dashboard so anyone can see and test all of this. One: the main dashboard. One button fetches Kalshi’s live price, reruns every step of the model, and shows Kalshi’s number next to ours, with all the charts behind them further down the page. Two: the Decision Lab. It runs the class model directly in the browser and offers two paths. Path A takes our recommendation. Path B lets you build your own forecast by choosing the emissions path, how sure you are about the climate, land-use emissions, the El Niño carry-over and whether 2049 counts. Three: your result. You get your own probability next to Kalshi’s, the distribution of your simulated futures (the same probability density idea, drawn as a histogram, with the futures that reach 2°C in green), and the Yes, No or Pass call from the rule on the last slide. With the recommended settings that’s 67% against Kalshi’s 70%: pass.
