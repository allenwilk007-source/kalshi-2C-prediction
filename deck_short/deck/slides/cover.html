Our question: what is the chance that any single year before 2050 is at least 2°C warmer than pre-industrial times? Kalshi, a regulated prediction market, has a contract on exactly that question. I’ll go in four steps. First, how we got our numbers: we took the climate model from our homework, simulated thousands of possible futures, and turned them into a probability density function. Second, how Kalshi gets its number, from traders buying and selling. Third, how the two compare. Fourth, what someone could hypothetically do with the difference. Then I’ll show the dashboard we built so you can try it yourself. Three numbers to keep in mind. Our model, run 72 different ways, says 81%. The same model with uncertainty widened to match the IPCC says 67%. Kalshi says 70%.
