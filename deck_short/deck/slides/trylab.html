Two more tools make the numbers concrete. Four: play the future. You choose Buy Yes, Buy No or Pass, then the Lab draws one of the simulated futures and reads the contract year by year, so you watch whether the bet pays. In this one the second-highest dataset reaches 2°C in 2042, so Yes wins $36 on $100. Play 100 futures at once and the odds turn into money: here 80 of 101 futures reached 2°C, close to the model’s 81%. Five: what moves the odds. Starting from your settings, the Lab changes one assumption at a time and ranks them. How sure we are about the climate matters most, 54% to 93%, then emissions, 44% to 75%. Land use and whether 2049 counts are worth about 5 points each, and the El Niño carry-over doesn’t matter for the 2040s. Click any option to switch to it. The Lab also has an odds explorer for any threshold and deadline, and a price slider that shows where your call flips if the market moves. It’s all hypothetical: nothing places a trade. Thank you.
