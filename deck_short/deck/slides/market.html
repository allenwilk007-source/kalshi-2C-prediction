2 · HOW KALSHI GETS ITS NUMBER

This is the real market on Kalshi, captured on 18 November 2026. The question: will the world pass 2°C over pre-industrial levels before 2050? Three things to read off the page. First, the price is the probability. A Yes contract pays one dollar if any calendar year before 2050 is at least 2°C above the 1850–1900 average. At the moment of this screenshot Yes cost 73 cents and No 32 cents, and Kalshi shows a 71.7% chance. Earlier that morning, when we took the order book for our analysis, it was 68 cents to sell and 72 to buy, about 70%. Prices move a little every day. Second, the rules. The market rules at the bottom say it resolves Yes when at least two of four agencies report a year at or above 2.0°C: NASA’s Goddard Institute, NOAA’s NCEI, Berkeley Earth and HadCRUT. That’s exactly the rule we built into our simulation. Third, it’s small. Only about $21,700 has traded since the market opened in June 2025. Look at the chart: in September one order pushed the price from the mid-70s down to about 50% for a moment. So the market’s number rests on very little money.
